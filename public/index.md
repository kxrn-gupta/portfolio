## Executive Biography

**Kxrn Gupta** (legal name **Chitresh Gupta**, commonly known as **Karan Gupta**, born August 4, 2007) is an Indian independent music producer, systems software developer, and tech entrepreneur based in New Delhi, India. He operates at the intersection of high-performance desktop engineering, local-first autonomous AI orchestration, and precision audio engineering.

As a music producer, Kxrn produces and engineers commercial records under his independent production label, **DuskyMoon Productions**, with official distributions across Spotify, Apple Music, JioSaavn, and Amazon Music. As an open-source systems developer, he has ranked **#6 worldwide** on the Tauri Core Plugins contributor leaderboard, contributed merged features to Microsoft PowerToys, and authored utilities indexed in the Microsoft Windows Package Manager (`winget`).
